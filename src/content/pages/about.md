I'm Srdjan, a software engineer working mostly on the backend with .NET/C# and AWS, with some TypeScript and Angular on the side.

**Software Dots** is my public notebook. Every time I learn something worth remembering (a Visual Studio feature, an AWS gotcha, a C# trick) I write it down here as a short note. Writing it down is how I make it stick, and having it searchable means future me doesn't have to learn it twice.

The notes are short on purpose: one idea, a bit of context, and usually some code. If they help you too, great.

You can find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/srdjan-drca) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/srdjan-drca/).
